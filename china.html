Welcome to China

China is one of the world's oldest civilizations, with ancient cities, famous landmarks, mountain scenery, and some of the most varied food anywhere. From the busy streets of Beijing and Shanghai to quiet villages and historic sites, it is a destination for travelers who love history, culture, and adventure.
