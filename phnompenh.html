Welcome to Phnom Penh

Phnom Penh is the capital of Cambodia, set where the Mekong and Tonlé Sap rivers meet. The city mixes French colonial buildings, golden temples, busy markets, and riverside cafes, and it has a rich and sometimes painful history that visitors can learn about.
