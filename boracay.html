Welcome to Boracay

Boracay is a small island in the Philippines famous for its powdery white sand, clear water, and beautiful sunsets. It is a popular destination for beach lovers, water sports fans, and anyone looking to relax by the sea.

Top things to do

A long stretch of fine white sand lined with restaurants, shops, and bars, and a favorite spot to watch the sunset.

A quieter beach on the northern end of the island, known for its shells and calmer atmosphere.

Take a boat trip around nearby spots for snorkeling, swimming, and exploring small islands.
