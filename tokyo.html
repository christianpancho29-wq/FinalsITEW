Welcome to Tokyo

Tokyo is the capital of Japan. It is famous for its advanced technology, colorful city lights, anime culture, delicious food, and ancient temples. It is a popular destination for tourists who want to experience both traditional Japanese culture and modern city life.

Top things to do

A famous pedestrian crossing surrounded by tall buildings and giant digital screens. It is a great destination for first-time visitors to Tokyo, photography enthusiasts, and travelers who want to experience Japan's modern urban culture. You can visit the crossing itself for free, while observation decks and other attractions may require tickets.

Travel Tip:

  • Visit in the evening to enjoy the illuminated billboards and lively city atmosphere. Be mindful of pedestrians and traffic, and avoid stopping in the middle of the crossing to take photos.

A tower offering beautiful panoramic views of the city. One of Japan's most famous landmarks and a major tourist attraction. Standing at 634 meters tall, it is a broadcasting tower with observation decks that offer spectacular views of Tokyo. Visitors can admire the city's impressive skyline, see Mount Fuji on clear days, and enjoy shopping and dining in the surrounding Tokyo Solamachi complex.

Senso-ji is a historic Buddhist temple in Asakusa, known for its traditional architecture and the famous Kaminarimon Gate.

Travel Tip:

  • Explore Nakamise shopping street and be respectful of visitors who are praying at the temple.
